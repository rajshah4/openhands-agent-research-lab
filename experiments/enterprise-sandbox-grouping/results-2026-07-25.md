## What this proves

- Enterprise grouping reuses compute without giving up first-class
  conversations or UI history.
- Fresh conversation context and distinct workspace archive paths can coexist
  inside one sandbox.
- At least two trusted agents can execute concurrently in the same sandbox on
  this deployment.
- Grouping reduced five conversation executions to one active sandbox.
- The supported V1 pause endpoint can release the grouped sandbox after the
  batch.

## What remains before production

The current worker owns sandbox pause per attempt. Grouping changes that
ownership boundary. A production pool needs:

- a lease or reference count per sandbox;
- a rule that prevents pause while any conversation is starting or running;
- per-sandbox concurrency limits;
- draining and recycling after a job count, age, memory threshold, or failure;
- tenant and trust-policy checks before assigning work;
- reconciliation between V1 sandbox state and the physical runtime;
- metrics for queue time, reuse rate, failures, memory growth, and cleanup.

The immediate recommendation is a pool of two or three grouped Enterprise
sandboxes for trusted research agents, starting at one concurrent conversation
per sandbox and raising the limit to two only after this same validation is
repeated under longer-running, file-mutating work.
